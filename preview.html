Our Heritage & Ethos

Born in Hyderabad.
Elevating Indian Menswear.

Jhonty Hub was founded on a simple conviction: modern Indian men shouldn't have to choose between relaxed daily comfort and architectural luxury.

The 320 GSM Standard

Standard fast-fashion t-shirts use 140–180 GSM fabrics that twist and thin out after three washes. At Jhonty Hub, our signature tees and overshirts are custom-milled at 320 GSM—delivering heavy, luxurious drape, clean boxy shoulders, and lifetime shape retention.

Every garment is cut and assembled in Telangana by master artisans, combining traditional tailoring with contemporary Tokyo streetwear geometry.
